Overhead — logo directions

Built from the product's own ideas: the plan view, and the nested container frames. App palette throughout.

A · Plan view — nested frames

the literal idea

Cloud → region → resource, seen from above. Dashed middle ring is the region frame's own border style, so the mark is a literal miniature of what the canvas draws.

B · Viewfinder — crop marks

looking down at something

Corner brackets frame the word the way a viewfinder frames a shot — and the way a container frames its resources. Reads as "the view from above" without drawing an eye or a camera.

C · Altitude — stacked layers

the cost reading

Layers narrowing as they descend — the containment tree in profile, and a stand-in for a cost bar. The most "product" of the three; the least literal about the name.

D · The O is the frame

wordmark only

The O becomes a container with one resource inside it. Cheapest to reproduce, and the mark and the word are the same object — but only works at wordmark size, so it still needs A or B as the square avatar.

Notes

Type is Archivo 600 at -0.6 tracking — the font already in the app, so the logo and the top bar match. For a final asset the wordmark should be converted to outlines so it renders identically without the font installed.

Colours are the app's tokens exactly: #3B82F6 accent, #00A4A6 region teal, #6FE3B0 good, #8FB8FF accent-ink. On light backgrounds the blue steps down to #2563EB for contrast.
